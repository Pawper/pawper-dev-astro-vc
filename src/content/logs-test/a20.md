I've kept a work log for the past two years. Not a diary — a record of what I did, what I decided, and what I was uncertain about. It started as a memory aid and has become something more useful: a way to read my own patterns.

Looking back six months, I can see the shape of my mistakes. I tend to underestimate integration work. I tend to abandon documentation right before I ship, when I'm most tired of the feature. I start too many things in parallel when I'm bored with the main task.

None of this is surprising in retrospect. But I couldn't have told you any of it before reading the logs. The data changes the self-model in a way that introspection alone doesn't.
