A half-shipped feature is worse than an unshipped one in a specific way: it creates expectations you haven't met yet. Users find it, assume it's complete, try to use it in ways you didn't intend, and form opinions based on an incomplete experience.

The solution isn't to never ship incrementally — incremental shipping is usually right. It's to be intentional about what the half-shipped state communicates. A feature that's clearly in beta, with a label saying so, creates a different expectation than one that appears finished.

The worst outcome is a feature that looks finished but isn't. The second worst is a feature that's genuinely finished but looks unfinished. Both are failures of communication, not of engineering.
